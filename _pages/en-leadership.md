My leadership work aims to strengthen speech-language pathology, advanced professional education and interdisciplinary care by connecting scientific societies, universities, clinical practice and health system needs.

## Leadership roles

- **President, Chilean Society of Swallowing and Feeding (SOCHIDA):** scientific and professional development in swallowing, feeding and person-centred care.
- **Past President, Intensive Care Speech-Language Pathology and Occupational Therapy Division, SOCHIMI:** development of both professions in critical care, early rehabilitation and interdisciplinary practice.

## Areas of contribution

- Professional development and advanced education in complex clinical settings.
- Evidence-based continuing education, courses and postgraduate diplomas.
- Collaboration with Medicine, Nursing, Occupational Therapy and other health disciplines.
- Academic and clinical networks for knowledge translation.
- Technical advice and collaboration with health and higher education institutions.
- Connecting research, clinical practice and public health needs.
