My research connects clinically relevant questions with rigorous epidemiological methods to inform patient care, professional education and health decisions.

## Research areas

### Swallowing and critical care
Assessment and rehabilitation of swallowing in people with critical illness, respiratory support and artificial airways, including post-extubation dysphagia, tracheostomy and protective mechanisms.

### Clinical epidemiology and health outcomes
Factors associated with clinical outcomes, intervention effectiveness, adherence and the use of data to support clinical and management decisions.

### Public health, rehabilitation and education
Access to care, clinical registries, prevention, professional education and the translation of evidence into policy, programmes and educational settings.

## Current interests

- Swallowing during high-flow nasal cannula oxygen therapy.
- Post-extubation dysphagia and functional recovery in intensive care.
- Tracheostomy, cough, communication and airway protection.
- Rehabilitation models and clinical outcomes in complex care.
- Clinical registries, functional measurement and data-informed decisions.
- Population prevention and evaluation of health interventions.

## Methods

Systematic and scoping reviews, clinical observational studies, registry and database analysis, clinical and applied epidemiology, intervention evaluation, health education research and interdisciplinary projects.
