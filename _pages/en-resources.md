Academic, professional and educational resources supporting clinical practice, teaching and health education. The linked materials are available in Spanish.

- [Speech-language pathology and Alzheimer's disease](/alzheimer-fonoaudiologia/): communication, participation and swallowing guidance.
- [Alzheimer's family toolkit](/kit-alzheimer/): printable communication strategies, warning signs and a personal support profile.
- [Tracheostomy card](/carnet-traqueostomia/): a personalised printable record of the tube, procedure and care team.
- [Communication and aphasia card](/carnet-afasia/): communication support for everyday life, healthcare and emergencies.
- [Professional resources](/recursos-profesionales/): presentations, clinical materials and selected references.
- [Postgraduate programmes in Chile](/diplomados-fonoaudiologia/): a searchable directory.
- [Patient and caregiver resources](/recursos-pacientes/).
- [Student resources](/recursos-alumnos/).
- [Aphasia educational guide](/afasia/).
- [Swallowing and chronic respiratory disease](/voz-deglucion/).
- [CLAD Cancún 2026](/clad-cancun-2026/).

These educational materials support learning and do not replace individual assessment or advice from the treating team.
