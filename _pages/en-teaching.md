My teaching connects knowledge, reasoning and practice through clinical problems, data, evidence and real health situations. The aim is to help students and professionals make informed decisions.

## Teaching areas

- Adult communication, language, speech and swallowing disorders.
- Epidemiology and public health.
- Biostatistics and interpretation of evidence.
- Physiology and research methods.
- Critical care speech-language pathology and adult deglutology.

## Undergraduate education

Teaching in Speech-Language Pathology, Nursing and the Public Health Minor at Universidad de los Andes, with methodological supervision of student research.

## Postgraduate and continuing education

Academic direction and teaching in the **Diploma in Intensive Care Speech-Language Pathology** and the **Diploma in Adult Deglutology**.

## Teaching approach

Applied learning, case analysis, evidence interpretation, data use, simulation and assessment focused on progressive competency development.
