Speech-Language Pathologist with a Master's degree in Epidemiology. My career integrates adult rehabilitation, critical care, university teaching, applied research, public health and professional leadership.

## Education

- Master's degree in Epidemiology, Universidad de los Andes.
- Professional qualification in Speech-Language Pathology, Universidad del Desarrollo, 2009.
- Bachelor's degree in Speech-Language Pathology, Universidad del Desarrollo, 2008.

## Academic experience

- Assistant Professor (Extraordinary appointment), School of Speech-Language Pathology, Universidad de los Andes.
- Instructor, School of Nursing, Universidad de los Andes.
- Director, Diploma in Adult Deglutology.
- Director, Diploma in Intensive Care Speech-Language Pathology.
- Previous undergraduate teaching at Universidad Internacional SEK and teaching assistantships and clinical education at Universidad del Desarrollo.

## Clinical and professional experience

- **Instituto Nacional del Tórax:** critical care and inpatient rehabilitation; swallowing, airway care, sleep medicine and endoscopic swallowing assessment in collaboration with Otolaryngology.
- **Hospital del Salvador, Neurology Service:** inpatient and outpatient care in swallowing, speech, language and cognition; cognitive neurology and memory clinic work.
- **LANNEC:** cognitive assessment in dementia and primary progressive aphasia.
- **Servicio de Salud Arauco:** occupational health, absence management, waiting lists and staff training.
- **Private practice:** assessment and intervention in adult speech, language, swallowing, voice and cognition.

## Leadership and service

President of SOCHIDA and former President of the Intensive Care Speech-Language Pathology and Occupational Therapy Division of SOCHIMI. Experience in research and clinical ethics committees, technical advisory panels and invited editorial review.

## Skills

Clinical epidemiology, data analysis, evidence synthesis, health outcomes, university teaching, programme design, health management and interdisciplinary collaboration.
